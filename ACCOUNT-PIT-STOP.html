Before we begin

Create or use an account only if your teacher and district have approved it. Use a non-identifying username, verify your email, and keep recovery information private. GitHub Education verification is helpful, but it is not required for the first build.

No account yet? Join a working team as navigator, designer/tester, or documentarian. Never share credentials or use someone else’s account.

Teacher release: 2026.08-pilot. The template is a snapshot, not a live update stream.

1 · Open the starting point

  1. Open the public Coding Club Build Lab template or the template-generation link from Classroom.
  2. Your team captain chooses Use this template and creates one private repository named for the team.
  3. The captain invites teammates and the teacher. Everyone accepts with their own account.

2 · Open the lab

  1. Open the team repository and choose Code → Codespaces → Create codespace on main.
  2. Wait for the launcher to appear. If the preview is not visible, open the Ports panel and open port 8000.
  3. Keep two tabs open: the Codespace editor for code and Mission Control for directions.
Four things, four jobs: GitHub holds the repository, Codespace is the editor, Mission Control explains the mission, and the forwarded preview shows the result.

3 · Make the first signal

  1. Open Copilot Chat if it is available and ask: Which file controls the product title? Do not edit anything. Give us one next step.
  2. In the editor, open meetings/01-ridiculous-website/project/index.html.
  3. Change the heading, save, and watch the preview. AI is optional; a peer or teacher can answer the same question.

4 · Prove the connection

  1. Open Source Control, write Day 0: account setup test, and commit.
  2. Choose Sync Changes, then verify the commit appears on GitHub.
  3. Stop the Codespace when your teacher says the team is ready.

Green means ready for Day 1

Yellow is still participation. If an invitation or Copilot is unresolved, take a navigator, designer/tester, or documentarian role while your teacher repairs access. A Codespaces or preview failure is the priority to fix before Day 1.
