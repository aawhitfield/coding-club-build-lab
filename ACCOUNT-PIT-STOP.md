# Account Pit Stop · Day Zero

Complete this supervised 30-minute setup before Meeting 1. Your teacher will tell you which steps are allowed by district policy. Do not create an account or enter information that your school does not approve.

## Before Day Zero

1. Create or verify a personal GitHub account if you are eligible and approved.
2. Use a non-identifying username; do not put your full name in a project file.
3. Verify the account email and keep recovery information private.

GitHub Education verification is helpful but is not required for Day 1. Your teacher will provide a no-AI fallback if Copilot is not yet available.

## During the 30-minute pit stop

1. Open the public teacher template: <https://github.com/aawhitfield/coding-club-build-lab>
2. The team captain creates one private repository from the template.
3. The captain invites teammates and the teacher as collaborators.
4. Every teammate accepts the invitation using their own account.
5. One teammate opens a two-core Codespace and port 8000 preview.
6. Open Copilot Chat and ask: “Which file controls the product title? Do not edit anything. Give us one next step.”
7. Change one heading, save, and verify the preview.
8. Commit as `Day 0: account setup test`, sync, verify the commit on GitHub, and stop the Codespace.

## Team status

Your teacher will mark your team Green, Yellow, or Red. Yellow students still participate as navigator, designer/tester, or documentarian while access is repaired. No student shares credentials.

Never share passwords, recovery codes, tokens, or another student’s credentials. If your account is not ready, join a working team as navigator, designer/tester, or documentarian; you still participate fully.

GitHub’s student benefits have age and policy requirements. Your teacher will confirm eligibility before account creation.
