# Story Challenge Cards

- The substitute teacher is secretly an alien.
- Your Chromebook begins giving mysterious instructions.
- The cafeteria food becomes sentient.
- You are locked inside school after dark.
- The school mascot asks for your help.
- A routine fire drill opens a portal.

## Builder cards

- Add a third choice.
- Add a page that looks like a warning.
- Add a hidden link in ordinary text.
- Give the player two different kinds of success.
