## Challenge levels

- **Remix:** Finish the baseline with an original machine, meaningful image and alt text, matching `theme-backdrops/` background, and two lists.
- **Builder:** Add a second category button, a third list, or a visual mood class.
- **Boss Level:** Prevent an immediate repeat or coordinate a result object with its visual mood.
