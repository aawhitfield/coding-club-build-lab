# Chaos Button Inspiration and Challenge Cards

## Inspiration

- **Suspicious Excuse Machine:** combine a reason with an impossible detail.
- **Cafeteria Fortune Teller:** predict a lunch event and its consequence.
- **Emergency Side Quest:** generate a mission and a strange constraint.
- **Random School Superpower:** pair a power with an inconvenient weakness.
- **Questionable Club Mascot:** mix an animal, object, and dramatic title.
- **Villain Name Laboratory:** combine a title with a suspicious hobby.
- **After-School Decision Machine:** choose a plan and an unexpected complication.
- **Terrible Advice Department:** generate advice that is funny but safe.

## Challenge levels

### Remix

Complete the required baseline and make the title, meaningful image and alt text, `background-image`, colors, button, and result lists unmistakably yours. Choose a backdrop that belongs to the same world as your theme.

### Builder

Add a second category button, a third list, or a result mood that changes the page styling.

### Boss Level

Prevent an immediate repeat or coordinate a result object with its visual mood. Add only code the team can explain.
