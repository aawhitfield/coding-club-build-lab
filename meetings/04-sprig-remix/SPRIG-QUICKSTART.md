# Sprig Quickstart

1. Open the Sprig editor: <https://sprig.hackclub.com/>
2. Sign in using the approved student account method.
3. Open the starter game or choose **Remix** on a gallery game.
4. Press **Run** after every small change.
5. Change only one thing at a time.
6. Save according to the current Sprig interface; do not publish publicly unless your teacher has separately approved it.
7. Copy the final code into this meeting folder so the team has a backup.

If login or saving is blocked, continue with the teacher’s backup project and record the issue in `TEAM-LOG.md`. Do not share account credentials.
