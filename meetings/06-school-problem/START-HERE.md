# Meeting 6 · Fix a School Annoyance

## Today’s promise

You will design a small, safe prototype for a problem that students actually recognize.

## Were you here last time?

**YES:** Open the project your team started and review the problem brief.

**NO:** Open `catch-up/index.html`, change the title, audience, and one navigation label, test it, and then choose a team role.

1. Read `safe-project-examples.md`.
2. Choose a small problem, not a whole school system.
3. Complete `problem-brief.md` and `wireframe.md` before coding.
4. Open `project/index.html`; new students may use `catch-up/index.html`.
5. Transform the starter’s screens and labels.

## Safe boundaries

Do not collect real student information, require school-system access, request passwords, track locations, or claim official school endorsement. Use sample data.

## Challenge levels

- **Remix:** transform the starter into a clickable prototype.
- **Builder:** add a screen, input, reveal, or recommendation.
- **Boss Level:** plan or begin a calculator, sorter, or local preference.

## Re-entry path

The catch-up app is complete. Change its title, audience, and one navigation label, then join a team.
