# Meeting 8 · Build Lab Showcase

## Today’s promise

Every student will help present, test, or explain a project.

## Were you here last time?

**YES:** Open your team's project and choose a contribution you can demonstrate.

**NO:** Join a team as tester, presenter, or documentarian, or open `catch-up-showcase-card/index.html` and prepare one learning card.

1. Choose the project your team most enjoys explaining.
2. Open `demo-planner.md` and write a two-minute explanation.
3. Verify that the link or preview works from a fresh tab.
4. Rehearse once without reading the code word-for-word.
5. Give specific feedback to another team.

## Required explanation

- What did the starter originally do?
- What did the team change?
- What feature did the team add?
- What code pattern does someone on the team now understand?
- What failed or changed during testing?
- What did each teammate contribute?

## Re-entry path

Students with no earlier project can join as tester, presenter, or documentarian, or complete `catch-up-showcase-card/index.html`.
