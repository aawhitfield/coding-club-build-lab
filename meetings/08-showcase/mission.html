Essential question

How can we show what we built so another person can understand, use, and extend it?

  1. Run the launch checkMake the audience’s first click work.

    Open the artifact from the selector. Try the main interaction in a fresh tab. If it is a Sprig game, open Sprig from the preview panel.

    CheckThe artifact loads, the main interaction responds, and the audience can restart or recover.
    RepairFix only the presentation bug that blocks the demo.

    Individual notice

    What should an audience member understand in the first ten seconds? Point to the exact screen, label, or game clue that teaches it.

  2. Plan the two-minute storyExplain purpose, changes, and evidence.

    Use this order: purpose → starter → team changes → interaction → test/revision → contributions.

    Purposewhy
    We made this for…

    Name the audience and moment.

    Patternhow
    When the user…, code…

    Explain one code connection.

    Evidenceproof
    We tested… so we changed…

    Show learning from a tester.

    Which part makes your demo an explanation rather than a feature tour?

  3. Rehearse without coachingMake the handoff smooth.

    Practice the demo once with another team. The audience should understand the purpose without the presenter explaining every click.

    Rehearsal test

    1. Start with the project closed or at its opening screen.
    2. Give the audience the first action, then let them try.
    3. Stop at two minutes and ask what they remember.
    4. Repair one confusing sentence, label, or transition.
  4. Gallery walk and feedbackGive feedback another builder can use.

    For three projects, record one sentence for each:

    I noticed…

    Name a specific choice or behavior.

    I wondered…

    Ask a curious, non-judgmental question.

    One next test…

    Suggest a small experiment, not a rewrite.

  5. Claim your contributionCelebrate growth, teamwork, and recovery.

    Complete the team log and demo notes. Commit with Meeting 8: showcase, sync, verify, and stop the Codespace.

    Reflection: “At the start I thought ____. Now I can ____. The next thing I am curious about is ____.”

    Stuck ladder and catch-up route

    1. Open the artifact in a fresh tab and name the first thing that fails.
    2. Try the saved backup or catch-up showcase card.
    3. Ask a tester or teammate for one specific next check.
    4. After your contribution is documented, optionally ask AI to tighten the explanation; it may not invent work.

    If you have no earlier project, test three artifacts or present the strongest feedback you observed. You belong in the broadcast.
